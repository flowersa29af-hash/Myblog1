The gondoliers of Venice are the iconic symbols of the city, expertly navigating the flat-bottomed gondola boats through narrow and shallow waterways. Gondola boats through narrow and shallow waterways.

Picture3 of Venice

It is an incredibly exclusive elite profession. The city restricts the number of active licenses to just 425. Aspiring gondoliers must complete 400 hours of training, clear rigorous history and foreign language exams, and pass a practical rowing test before an official panel of judges.

Picture of Venice

Ride Regulations: Official daytime city tariffs are fixed at €90 for a standard 30-minute private ride. Prices bump up to €110 for evening slots after 7:00 PM.
